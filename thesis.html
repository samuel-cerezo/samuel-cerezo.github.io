State Initialization and Estimation in Multimodal SLAM

Samuel Adrian Cerezo Jamed

University of Zaragoza · 2026

This dissertation investigates robust state initialization and estimation in multimodal SLAM, including visual-inertial and GNSS-inertial initialization, dense RGB-D-inertial estimation, and visual-inertial SLAM in deformable scenes.
